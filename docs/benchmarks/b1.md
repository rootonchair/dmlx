# B1 — Measurements (Oct 2026)

Machine: MacBook with Apple **M5, 24 GB**, macOS 26.5. Measured under real everyday load: other apps
open, **~5 GB available, ~11 GB already in swap, memory pressure "warn" at baseline**. That is a
harsher starting point than a freshly booted 16 GB Mac, which makes it a useful stand-in for the
16 GB target. 16 GB tiering was additionally exercised with `DMLX_EMULATE_TOTAL_GB=16`.

Models: 4-bit mflux packs from `mflux-community` (Apache-2.0).

## Component sizes (resident, measured)

| Model | text encoder | transformer | VAE | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLUX.2 Klein 4B q4 | 2.11–2.26 GB | 2.03 GB | 0.15 GB | 4.3 GB |
| Z-Image Turbo q4 | 2.11–2.26 GB | 3.23 GB | 0.15 GB | 5.5 GB |

Loading a component has no transient overhead (peak = resident after load).

## Working memory per phase, 1024×1024, beyond resident weights

| Model / task | encode | denoise | decode (exact) | decode (tiled) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Klein 4B t2i | +0.84 GB | +1.35 GB | **+8.4 GB** | +2.4 GB |
| Klein 4B edit (1 reference) | +0.84 GB | +3.5 GB | +8.4 GB | +2.4 GB |
| Z-Image Turbo t2i | +1.1 GB | +0.8 GB | **+6.6 GB** | +2.0 GB |

**The VAE decode is the single biggest risk**: an exact decode at 1024² briefly needs more than
the whole model. On a 16 GB Mac this alone causes swapping. Policy adopted:

- Macs under 32 GB always decode tiled.
- Above 32 GB, exact decode only when headroom is ≥ 1.5× its spike.
- Quality: Z-Image tiled vs exact differs by ~2/255 mean (visually identical). Klein shows a slight
  color-temperature shift (~8/255 mean), no seams.

## Speed (M5, 4-bit, warm)

| Model / task | steps | denoise | total turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| Klein 4B t2i 512² | 4 | ~1.2 s/step | ~6–10 s |
| Klein 4B t2i 1024² | 4 | ~3.1–3.3 s/step | ~15–16 s |
| Klein 4B edit 1024² (1 ref) | 4 | ~12 s/step | ~50–55 s |
| Z-Image Turbo t2i 1024² | 9 | ~5.4 s/step | ~50 s |

Decision: **Klein 4B is the default for both generate and edit.** It is 3× faster than Z-Image
for t2i here, and one model for both tasks means alternating generate/edit turns never swap
models on a 16 GB Mac. Z-Image stays available as an optional higher-detail model.

## No-hang runs (`dmlx bench`, probe running alongside)

Probe = separate process sampling a 50 ms timer and a tiny GPU job (proxy for UI compositing).
Gate: pressure never critical, swap-in rate ≤ 64 MB/s (pages read back from swap = what makes a Mac
stutter), GPU probe p99 ≤ 250 ms, timer p99 ≤ 50 ms.

### Final policy (tiled decode < 32 GB, file-cache-only availability, default MLX buffers)

| Case | strategy | loads | peak MLX | swap-in | GPU p99 | timer p99 | pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Klein t2i 1024 run 1 (cold) | eager | vae, te, transformer | 6.7 GB | 0.4 MB/s | 1.7 ms | 10 ms | ✅ |
| Klein t2i 1024 run 2 (warm) | eager | — | 6.7 GB | 12.9 MB/s | 1.7 ms | 10 ms | ✅ |
| Klein edit 1024, 16 GB tier, run 1 | eager | — | 7.8 GB | 10.5 MB/s | 2.9 ms | 10 ms | ✅ |
| Klein edit 1024, 16 GB tier, run 2 | eager | — | 7.8 GB | 2.9 MB/s | 2.6 ms | 10 ms | ✅ |

Peak MLX = resident weights (4.3 GB) + the largest phase (edit denoise +3.5 GB). That fits a
16 GB Mac with room for the system and foreground apps.

### What went wrong on the way (kept for the record)

1. **Exact VAE decode** (+8.4 GB at 1024²) pushed early runs to 10.5–12.6 GB peaks. Those runs
   moved ~8 GB of other apps' memory into swap on this machine (swap 11 GB → 19.6 GB). Fixed by
   tiling below 32 GB.
2. **"Available" counted other apps' inactive memory.** Taking it forces compression/swap. One edit
   planned as *eager* under that estimate was stopped by the guard ("system started swapping").
   Fixed: available = free + speculative + purgeable + file cache only.
3. **Short Metal command buffers** (`MLX_MAX_OPS_PER_BUFFER=32`, `MLX_MAX_MB_PER_BUFFER=256`),
   added for responsiveness, made working memory ~4× larger (denoise +5.6 GB vs +1.35 GB; tiled
   decode +8.6 GB vs +2.4 GB) with no measurable responsiveness gain (GPU probe p99 under 3 ms
   either way). Now opt-in (`--short-buffers`), off by default.
4. Swap *growth* turned out to be a poor hang signal (macOS swaps idle apps out on its own);
   the guard now aborts on sustained **swap-in** rate and critical pressure, with a 4 GB
   swap-growth backstop.

Responsiveness was never the problem: the GPU probe stayed under 4 ms p99 in every run.
**Memory, not GPU contention, is what threatens a hang**, and the decode spike is its main source.
